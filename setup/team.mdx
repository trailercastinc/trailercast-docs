## Inviting a teammate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Invite a teammate">
    On the Security & Permissions page, below the list of members.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter their email and send">
    Type the teammate's work email and click **Send invite**. Every invite joins as a **Member** unless you choose otherwise (see the note below).
  </Step>

  <Step title="They get an email">
    One-click signup link that ties them into your company workspace, good for 7 days. They land on the same setup flow but skip the company-creation step. The invite sits under **Pending invites** until they accept, and you can revoke it from there.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make them an Admin, if you want to">
    Once they have accepted, click **Promote** beside their name in the member list. **Demote** reverses it. Global Admin is always limited to one person and moves only by transfer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Rolling out workspace by workspace from 2026-11-18: a **Role** dropdown beside the email field lets an admin invite someone straight in as **Admin**, skipping the promote step. If you don't see the dropdown, it isn't enabled for your workspace yet. Invite them, then promote.
</Note>

Only an admin can invite another admin. A Member who has been given the invite permission can invite Members.

<Tip>
  Invited teammates inherit your tier, you don't need to upgrade their account separately.
</Tip>

## Seats

There's no seat cap. Plans are priced per seat, so each teammate you invite is a seat on your subscription. To add or remove seats, use **Settings → Billing → Contact us** and the team will adjust your subscription; see [Billing](/billing).

## Permissions matrix

The Security & Permissions page shows a checkbox grid of every granular permission for every teammate. Hover any row for an explanation of what that permission unlocks.

Common permissions:
- `demos.upload`, can upload new demos
- `demos.view_team`, can see other reps' demos
- `spaces.create`, can create Buyer Spaces
- `settings.brand_edit`, can change brand settings
- `team.manage`, can invite + remove teammates

Global Admin can revoke a permission AND lock it, meaning the affected user's matrix shows that row greyed-out and uneditable.

## Transferring Global Admin

If you want to hand off the company to a teammate (sale, role change, vacation):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Settings → Security & Permissions → Transfer Global Admin">
    Button only visible if you ARE the Global Admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the new GA from the dropdown">
    Must be an existing teammate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Transfer is immediate. You become Admin; they become GA.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Transferring Global Admin is irreversible without the new GA's cooperation. Pick someone you trust.
</Warning>
